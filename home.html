    Everything a lawyer would look up.

    Each report compiles the official records of one parcel into a single document. The excerpts below come from real reports.

    01 · The verdict

    Risk summary

    The report opens with a clear rating and every finding in plain language: what was found, why it matters, and what to do about it.

    02 · The verdict

    At a glance

    One page with the key facts and the status of every check: property number, plan, area, fiscal value, owners, encumbrances, access, zoning, water, and taxes.

    03 · The land

    Maritime zone (ZMT)

    For coastal parcels, the official ZMT demarcation on satellite imagery: the mojón markers and zona-pública boundary, the computed 150 m zona restringida, and how much of the parcel falls inside.

    04 · The land

    Protected areas

    The parcel is checked against SINAC’s registry of national parks, reserves, and refuges. Any overlap is mapped and measured; overlap with a state-owned category can bar private titling.

    05 · The land

    Bodies of water

    Rivers, quebradas, and lakes from the national hydrography, with their statutory protection strips. The report measures how much of the parcel is affected; building inside the strips is prohibited.

    06 · The land

    Natural hazardsNew

    The parcel checked against CNE’s flood, landslide and fault maps on satellite imagery, with the district’s volcanic alert on the day of the report.

    07 · The land

    Cadastral plan

    The registered survey (plano catastrado) with its number, date, surveyed area, and the scanned plan itself.

    08 · The land

    Road access

    Public road frontage confirmed against the national road network on satellite imagery, with the computed frontage — plus registered access easements (servidumbres) when the parcel depends on one. A parcel without legal access is flagged as critical.

    09 · The land

    Neighboring parcels & overlaps

    The parcel among its neighbors in the cadastre, on satellite imagery. Overlapping parcels and newly filed survey plans are measured and flagged.

    10 · The title

    Owners and their rights

    Every registered owner with their identification, type of right, and share: full ownership, fractional shares, bare ownership, and usufruct. A transfer requires every right-holder to sign.

    11 · The title

    Liens & encumbrances

    Mortgages, embargoes, easements, and other charges registered against the title, each explained in plain terms, together with court and administrative annotations.

    12 · The title

    Registry movements

    Every act registered against the property since 1994: transfers, mortgages constituted and canceled, subdivisions, and the parcel’s lineage from its mother finca.

    13 · Use & value

    Zoning & land use

    The canton’s plan regulador and the parcel’s zone with its building rules: permitted uses, coverage, height, setbacks, minimum lot size.

    14 · Use & value

    Wells & water concessions

    Registered wells and water concessions on and near the parcel, with each well’s protection perimeter and what it means for drilling and building permits.

    15 · Use & value

    Municipal taxes & valuation

    The declared fiscal value, the annual canon on a concession, the luxury-home-tax check, and the payment status of every municipal charge, including overdue balances.

    16 · Use & value

    Points of interest

    Supermarkets, schools, medical facilities, banks, and beaches near the parcel, with distances and ratings, summarized in an amenity score.
