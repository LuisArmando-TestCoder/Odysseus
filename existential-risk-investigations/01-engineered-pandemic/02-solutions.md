# 02 — Solutions: Homeostatic & Homeodynamic, Three Horizons

Legend: **[H]** homeostatic, **[D]** homeodynamic.

## Short term (0–5y) — homeostatic holds the line

- **S1 [H] Universal nucleic-acid synthesis screening.** Blocklist + anomaly detection on every DNA/RNA synthesis order globally, gating R1's input flow. *Benchmarks:* financial fraud monitoring; package-registry security scanning.
- **S2 [H] Metagenomic biosurveillance network.** Wastewater + air + sentinel-clinic sequencing at daily cadence, global coverage — a seismic-style "fire alarm." Attacks detection/characterization delay. *Benchmarks:* seismic early-warning arrays; network IDS/IPS.
- **S3 [H] Platform vaccines + broad-spectrum antivirals, pre-approved and pre-positioned.** mRNA/self-amplifying platforms with regulatory pathways that swap in a new antigen without a fresh trial; host-target antivirals that resist escape. Strengthens B3. *Benchmarks:* the Linux "software-update" patch model.
- **S4 [H] Biosafety 4.0 lab regime.** Verified isolation, no-blame incident reporting, single accountability, "black box" audit. Attacks accidental/bioerror failure modes. *Benchmark:* aviation just-culture + crash investigation.
- **S5 [H] Buffers & pre-positioned manufacturing.** PPE, antivirals, raw material, modular fill-finish lines distributed regionally. *Benchmark:* JIT-vs-resilience in supply-chain engineering.

## Medium term (5–20y) — homeodynamic emerges

- **M1 [D] Intrinsic safety / incapable-by-design biology.** Recoded organisms, xenobionts, nutritional/structural dependency chasms, orthogonal genetic codes — engineered biology that *cannot propagate* outside its intended chassis. Changes the paradigm: the tool is safe even in hostile hands. *Benchmarks:* passively-safe reactors; memory-safe languages.
- **M2 [D] Artificial immune system for humanity.** Agnostic, anticipatory defense engines: in-silico antigen design, universal presentation platforms, metered micro-manufacture co-located with populations. A self-tuning defense that evolves with the threat. *Benchmarks:* the literal adaptive immune system; infrastructure-as-code.
- **M3 [D] Global bio-governance with verification.** A "CERN/IAEA for biology": shared observatories, *open knowledge, verified instantiation* (synthesis is tracked; knowledge is not). *Benchmarks:* IAEA safeguards; WADA anti-doping.
- **M4 [D] Differential disclosure / information-hazard governance.** Dual-use research disclosed defensively; capability-enabling synthesis gated or escrowed; publication time-embargoed. *Benchmark:* coordinated vulnerability disclosure; crypto export-control history.

## Long term (20–50y+) — homeodynamic dominates

- **L1 [D] Planetary immune-surveillance loop.** Closed-loop detect → design → deploy → verify at hour-scale latency, continuously self-validating. *Benchmarks:* post-2008 macroprudential systemic-risk monitoring; space situational awareness.
- **L2 [D] Civilization-level redundancy.** Reduce single points of failure: distributed energy/food/pharma independence; ultimately off-world and biodome refuges ("disaster-recovery for the species"). *Benchmark:* geo-redundant infrastructure; fault-tolerant distributed systems.
- **L3 [D] Self-limiting technology governance.** Anticipatory/reflexive institutions with *hard technical* brakes (intrinsic, not policy-only) and "circuit breakers" that trip on warning signals. *Benchmarks:* stock-exchange circuit breakers; the autonomic nervous system.
- **L4 [D] Goal/paradigm shift.** Planetary resilience re-cast as a *competitive, prestige-conferring* good, so coordination becomes self-interested. *Benchmarks:* the Montreal Protocol; ISO/ITU standards bodies.

**Design principle:** homeostatic measures (S1–S5) buy time and hold the line while the homeodynamic layer (M1–M2, L1–L3) matures; the homeodynamic layer is what actually removes the tail. See `04-benchmarks-and-synthesis.md`.
