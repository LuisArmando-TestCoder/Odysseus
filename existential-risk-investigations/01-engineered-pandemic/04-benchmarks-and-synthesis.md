## 2. Synthesis — the organizing insight

The threat is **intelligence entering a forbidden corner of biology's fitness landscape**, scaling with capability diffusion (R1). Natural pandemics self-terminate (B1); engineered ones may not. Therefore the strategy must:

1. **Slow and make-visible entry into the forbidden corner** (homeostatic: screening, disclosure, intrinsic-safety cost-raisers) — attacks R1/R2.
2. **Win the delay race** (homeostatic: hour-scale detection + pre-committed agnostic response) — attacks the OODA-vs-generation-time gap.
3. **Replace fixed defense with co-evolving defense** (homeodynamic: artificial immune system, intrinsic safety, planetary loop) — the layer that actually removes the tail.

**Homeodynamics is not an optional flourish; it is the necessary response to a non-stationary threat.** A purely homeostatic biosecurity (detect-and-damp a static baseline) is structurally doomed, because the threat's "setpoint" — what can be built, and how cheaply — keeps moving. The system itself must be built to keep re-tuning.

## 3. Confidence & open questions

- **High confidence:** capability-cost decline continues; detection delay is the cheapest leverage.
- **Medium:** intrinsic safety achievable at scale; coordination achievable in a coalition-of-the-willing.
- **Open:** whether the extinction tail is real (drives everything); whether agnostic countermeasures can keep pace; governance of the homeodynamic layer itself — *who tunes the tuner?*
